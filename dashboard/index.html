Clip Curator

An AI video pipeline decides which clips are fit for a training dataset, with no human review. Object detection, scene labels and signal checks become data; governed rules keep or cut every clip, and every decision is scored against known answers.

How it is made

  1. CutReal footage is cut into six-second clips, and a known defect is planted in about a third of them with a fixed seed.
  2. ExtractYOLOv8 finds objects, CLIP labels each scene from reference clips, and signal checks measure exposure, focus, motion, hashes and file integrity.
  3. LoadAll of it lands raw in DuckDB or Snowflake.
  4. Decidedbt applies the governed rules; every clip is kept or cut with its reason.
  5. ScoreRules and labels are tested against the answer key; the build fails if they slip. make verify re-runs the models and checks every output matches byte for byte.
